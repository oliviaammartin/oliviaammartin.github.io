### Nuclear Quantum Effects at Water-Mineral Interfaces

Nuclear quantum effects (NQEs) introduce significant structural and dynamic differences between water and heavy water at the α-alumina mineral interface, compared to their bulk counterparts. In collaboration with Prof. Eric Borguet at Temple University, we find that interfacial O-H and O-D vibrational frequencies and relaxation pathways, such as intermolecular coupling and spectral diffusion, of water and heavy water differ significantly at the mineral surface, highlighting the **importance of NQEs in understanding the water's properties at water-alumina interface**.

### Thermodynamics of Lipid Assembly Formations

Simple lipids are known to spontaneously aggregate to form micelles or bilayers. Could these lipids form micelles or bilyers at mineral surfaces? *How? Why?* Using computational molecular dynamics (MD) simulations and enhanced sampling techniques, we model simple, single chain lipids at different hydrophilic mineral surfaces. We find that **bulk water, interfacial water, and the mineral surface itself play unique roles in how lipids adsorb and form micelles** at water-mineral interfaces by calculating the thermodynamic driving forces, including how H-bonding and hydrophobic driving forces differ between crystal cuts of α-alumina.
